Where you are

Geolocation was built and measured with a stand-in position. No position from this Mac has reached a page yet.





The first question


  1. Press getCurrentPosition.
  2. Answer macOS's own prompt if it comes, then Savoia's bar.


macOS asks once for the app; Savoia's bar asks for this site; then coordinates that are really where you are.



The answer is remembered, and can be taken back


  1. Reload, press getCurrentPosition again.
  2. Open the site menu under the lock and switch location off; press again.


no bar the second time; after switching off, PERMISSION_DENIED at once.



Watching


  1. Allow again, press watchPosition, leave it for half a minute, press stop.


say what you see: how often a line arrives on a Mac that is not moving. Once a second is what was built for the stand, and may be too much.



With Location Services off for Savoia


  1. System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services: switch Savoia off. Press getCurrentPosition.
  2. Switch it back on.


POSITION_UNAVAILABLE, promptly — not a page left waiting.



In a private window


  1. Open this page in a private window and press getCurrentPosition.


the bar asks; the answer is gone when the private window closes.
