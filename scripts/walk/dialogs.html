A page's dialogs, under a hand

The sheets behind alert, confirm, prompt and the file chooser were rebuilt so that an agent can answer them. A self-test presses their buttons; it cannot press a real key or pick a file.

Escape on a confirm

  1. Press the button.
  2. When the sheet is up, press Esc.

the sheet goes away and the line below reads false.




Return on a confirm and on an alert


  1. Press each button, then Return.


confirm reads true; the alert just closes.







Typing in a prompt


  1. Press the button.
  2. Type a word into the field — in Latin letters, then again in Russian.
  3. Return. Then once more, and Esc instead.


what you typed appears below; after Esc it reads null. The letters go into the field, not into the page or the tab bar.





A file picked by hand


  1. Press Choose File, pick any small text file, press the panel's own button.
  2. Again, and press Cancel. Again, and press Esc.


the name and the first line of the file appear; Cancel and Esc leave it as it was.







A dialog from a tab that is not in front


  1. Press the button, then switch to another tab within five seconds.


this is a question, not a test: say what happened — did a sheet cover the window while you were in the other tab, and is that what you want?





Closing a tab with a dialog up


  1. Open this page in a second tab. There, press the button and, with the sheet up, close that tab (⌘W may be taken by the sheet — use the tab's ×).


the sheet goes with its tab; nothing is left hanging over the window.
