Windows a page opens

A window opened with window.open is a tab with its opener now. Only the mechanics were measured, on a stand page.

window.open with no size

  1. Press the button.
  2. In what opens, press its button.

a tab opens saying its opener is present; its button writes a line below and the tab closes by itself.




window.open asking for a size


  1. Press the button, then the button in what opens.


the same, with an opener. Say whether it came as a tab or a window, and what size it reports — the asked size is known not to be honoured.





A window nobody asked for


  1. Press the button and wait three seconds without touching anything.


nothing opens: a window with no click behind it is blocked. The line says null.
