Fullscreen and picture-in-picture

The state, the size and the way home are measured. Whether anything is drawn is not — and a black screen was the fault the old workaround existed for.






The picture in fullscreen


  1. Press Play, then Fullscreen.
  2. Leave with Esc.


the moving picture fills the screen — not black — and the page is back as it was after Esc.



The page knows it is visible


  1. Enter fullscreen, wait three seconds, leave, and read the lines under the buttons.


every line says visible. A page that reads hidden while it fills the screen was seen once, with Savoia behind another app.



Fullscreen of something that is not a video


  1. Press "Fullscreen of the whole box", then Esc.


the box with its buttons fills the screen and comes back.



Leaving the page while in fullscreen


  1. Press "Fullscreen, then leave in 4 s" and wait.


fullscreen ends, and the tab shows the next page — not a blank tab. Go back afterwards: this page is drawn.



Picture in picture


  1. Press Play, then "Picture in picture" (or ⌥⇧P).
  2. Switch to another tab, then close this one.


a small floating player with the moving picture; it survives the tab switch, and goes away with the tab.
