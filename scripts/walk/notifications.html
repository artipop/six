Notifications

Built and measured without ever calling the system: no banner has been seen.





Asking


  1. Press "Ask permission". Answer macOS's own prompt if it comes, then Savoia's bar.


macOS asks once for the app, Savoia's bar asks for this site, and the line reads granted.



A banner


  1. Press "Show one".


a system banner with the title, the body and this site's name; the line show below.



A click on it


  1. Press "Show one in 5 s" and switch to another tab — or another app.
  2. Click the banner.


Savoia comes forward on this tab, and the line click is below.



Closing


  1. Show one, then press "Close the last".
  2. Show one and dismiss the banner yourself.


the banner goes when the page closes it; say whether close is written in each case.



An icon


  1. Press "Show one with an icon".


the banner carries an orange square with a dark border.



From a service worker, and a click that opens a tab


  1. Press "Show one from the service worker".
  2. Click the banner.
  3. Then once more: press it, quit nothing, but close this tab before clicking the banner.


a banner titled "From the service worker" with the orange icon; the click opens a new tab on the history station, and this page logs what the worker says. With this tab closed the click still opens the tab.



The worker of another profile


  1. Open this page in your other profile, allow there too, and press "Show one from the service worker". Click the banner.


the tab opens in the profile the notification came from, not in the one that happens to be in front.



Refused, and a private window


  1. Switch notifications off for this site in the menu under the lock; press "Show one".
  2. Open this page in a private window; press "Ask permission".


no banner, and error or nothing; in the private window denied with no question, as in Safari.



Two profiles, two answers


  1. Allow here. Open this page in your other profile and refuse there. Press "Show one" in each.


a banner from the profile that allowed, none from the one that refused.
