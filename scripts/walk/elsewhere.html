On real sites

Stand pages are written by the people who wrote the code. These need pages that were not.

Signing in through a window the site opens

  1. On a site you have an account on, use "Sign in with Google" (or Apple, or Telegram's widget).

the sign-in opens as a tab, finishes, closes itself, and the first site knows who you are.

A video site in fullscreen

  1. YouTube or the like: play, go fullscreen, then press "next" or let autoplay move on. Press Esc.

a picture throughout, never black; after Esc the tab shows the page, not nothing.

A map

  1. Open a maps site and press its "my location" button.

the bar asks, and the dot lands where you are; the map does not jitter while you sit still.

A chat that notifies

  1. Mattermost, or any web chat: allow notifications, switch to another app, have a message arrive.
  2. Click the banner.

a banner with the message; the click brings Savoia forward on the chat's tab and the chat opens the conversation.

A call

  1. Join a test call (Meet, Jitsi, Telemost). Mute the microphone from the address field, then from the site's own button. Share a window.

the others see and hear you; both mutes work and agree with each other; sharing shows the window you picked.

A board an agent can move things on

  1. On a Trello-like board, ask the agent on ⌘E to move a card to another column.

the card moves and stays moved after a reload. If it lets go halfway, say on which site.

An extension's popup and menu

  1. Click an installed extension's button in the toolbar; use its popup. Open its options page.

the popup hangs from its button and works; the options page opens as a tab.

A site with a broken certificate

  1. Open https://expired.badssl.com and https://self-signed.badssl.com.

Savoia's own page about the certificate, and no lock in the address field — or a struck one — while it is shown.
