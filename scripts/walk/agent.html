An agent's hand

Hover, drag, dialogs and files were measured through tools, with nobody watching the screen. Here you watch. Each check has a sentence to give the agent on the ⌘E line, with this tab in front.

  • An item inside the menu


  • One
  • Two
  • Three




Hover


  1. Ask: "Hover over the Menu button on this page and tell me what appears."


you see the menu open under the button and stay while the agent reads it; the agent names the item. Nothing is left open or stuck afterwards — move your own pointer over the page to check.



A list sorted by the pointer


  1. Ask: "Drag Alpha below Charlie in the first list."


the order becomes Bravo, Charlie, Alpha and the line below says so. Your own pointer is not carried away.



Drag and drop


  1. Ask: "Drag One onto the 'Drop here' box." Then: "Drag Two onto 'No drops here'."
  2. Do the first one yourself, by hand, afterwards.


the first writes dropped on the target: One; the second fails and the agent says the element takes no drop; your own drag works as it always did, and no ghost image is left on screen.



A dialog the agent answers


  1. Ask: "Press 'Raise a confirm' and accept what it asks."


the sheet goes up and comes down by itself; the line reads true. Say whether it reads as a flicker.



A file the agent attaches


  1. Make a file: echo walk > /tmp/walk.txt. Ask: "Attach /tmp/walk.txt to the file field on this page."
  2. Ask again with another file, having answered "always" the first time if the card offers it.


a card naming the file each time — also the second time; no file panel; the line shows the file's name and content.



The same with Savoia behind another app


  1. Give any of the requests above, then switch to another app and work there for a minute.


the agent finishes without pulling Savoia to the front, without moving your pointer, and nothing pops up over the app you are in. A Paste menu appearing over it is a known fault — say if you see it.
