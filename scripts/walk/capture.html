Camera, microphone, screen

The camera's question passes in wpt on a stand page. A real call, the screen picker and the mute buttons in the address field were not tried since the move to WKWebView.






The camera


  1. Press Camera and allow in the bar.


your picture on the left, and a camera button in the address field, red while live.



Mute from the address field


  1. Press Both. Click the camera button in the address field, then the microphone one.
  2. Click each again.


each mutes on its own — the picture freezes or goes dark, the lines muted and unmuted appear — and the call is not dropped.



Blocking while live


  1. With the camera on, open the menu under the lock and switch the camera off for this site.


the camera stops at once: ended below, and the light on the Mac goes out.



Sharing the screen


  1. Press "Share the screen" and pick a window in macOS's picker.
  2. Then "Stop everything".


the picked window on the right, a screen indicator in the address field while it lasts, gone after stopping.



An iPhone nearby


  1. Press Devices with your iPhone in reach, and again with it out of reach (or Continuity Camera off).


say which cameras are listed each time — one wpt test changes its answer with the phone, and this settles why.
